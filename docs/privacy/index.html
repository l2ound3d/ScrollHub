ScrollHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

ScrollHub (“the app”) is a CBZ comic reader for Android and Windows, published by the ScrollHub project. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Summary

ScrollHub is designed to read comics from files you choose. The app does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that send your reading activity to us.

Most data stays on your device. Network access is used only when you choose to connect to a network library (SMB).

Information the app stores on your device

When you use ScrollHub, the app may store the following locally in the app’s private data area:

Data Purpose
Reading progress Remembers the last page read for each comic
App settings Theme, reading mode, fit mode, reading direction, and similar preferences
Library location The folder or network path you selected for your library
Last opened comic Lets the app offer to resume where you left off
Network login details If you connect to an SMB/network share, the host, username, and password you enter are saved in settings so you do not have to re-enter them each time
Downloaded comic cache Temporary copies of CBZ files being read (for example from a network share or content URI) to improve performance

You can clear this data by clearing the app’s storage in Android system settings or by uninstalling the app.

Files and folders you choose

ScrollHub only accesses comic files and folders that you pick through the app (for example via Android’s document picker or a library folder on Windows). The app reads those files to display comics and to remember your place.

On Android, if you grant access to a folder or file, the app may request permission to keep that access across restarts so your library and reading progress continue to work.

We do not receive, upload, or store your comic files on our servers.

Network use

The app requests the Internet permission so it can connect to network libraries when you configure SMB/network browsing.

  • Network connections are initiated by you.
  • Comic data from network shares is downloaded to your device for reading and may be cached locally.
  • The app does not send your comics, library contents, or reading history to ScrollHub’s developers.

Information we do not collect

ScrollHub does not intentionally collect:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, or messages unrelated to comics you open
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking
  • Analytics about how you use the app

We do not sell personal information.

Third parties

  • Google Play — If you install the app from Google Play, Google’s own terms and privacy policy apply to your use of the store and related services.
  • Your file and network providers — Comics may come from your device storage, cloud storage apps you connect through the system picker, or network shares you configure. Those providers handle your data under their own policies.

The app does not embed third-party advertising or analytics libraries.

Children’s privacy

ScrollHub is a general-purpose comic reader and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security

Data processed by the app is stored on your device using the operating system’s app sandbox. Network credentials you save are stored in the app’s local settings file on your device. No method of storage is 100% secure; keep your device and app install secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
